Swimming Lessons

Affordable, safe, quality swimming lessons whether you are new to the world of swimming or an experienced water enthusiast, for ages 3 to adult.

  • New sessions start monthly
  • Progressive program from beginner to swim team levels
  • Low instructor-to-student ratio: 5:1 for ages 3–5, 6:1 or 8:1 for older children
  • Heated outdoor pools
  • Strong focus on technique at all levels
  • All instructors are American Red Cross Water Safety Instructors
  • Private 30-minute lessons by availability

Register by emailing swcswim@swccd.edu or stop by the Front Desk. Lessons break for Southwestern College district and federal holidays, with no make-ups.
