Aquatics

JAWS features the largest aquatics complex in the San Diego region: two Olympic-size pools and a warm-water instructional pool, home to the Jaguar swim and water polo teams and SOL Aquatics. Every time on these pages comes from the pools' live schedule, so when JAWS changes it, these pages change with it.

The pools

Fast Pool

  • 50 meters, deep throughout
  • Competition swimming and water polo
  • Jaguar meets and games

Flex Pool

  • 50 meters, variable depth
  • Diving, PE, recreation and instruction
  • A bulkhead splits it between programs

Teaching Pool

  • Warm-water instructional pool
  • Swim lessons and adaptive therapy

More than 1 million gallons of water, heated to 74–80°F. Opened January 2018.

Swimming pool policies

  • Swimming only when a lifeguard is on duty; follow lifeguards' directions.
  • Bathing suits, board shorts or rash guards only; no cotton or cutoffs. No deck changing.
  • Sunscreen 15 minutes before entering; swim diapers required for children not toilet trained.
  • No somersaults or back dives from the deck, blocks or bulkhead; no hanging on lane lines. Starting blocks are not for recreational use.
  • No glass on deck. Staff may close parts of the pool in bad weather.

What this replaces

  • Three sets of pool hours. The Aquatics page lists open lap swim as "M/W/Th 5:30 AM – 6:00 PM, T/F 5:30 AM – 7:00 PM, Sa 7:00 AM – 1:00 PM", while the same page's footer says "Monday & Wednesday 5:30 AM – 6:00 PM, Tuesday & Thursday 5:30 AM – 7:00 PM, Friday 5:30 AM – 8:00 PM", and the Contact page says "Monday – Friday 5:30 AM – 7:00 PM". (All quoted from swcjaws.com.) Here there is one schedule, and every page reads it.
  • Which pool? Three pools, and lap swim never says which one or how many lanes. Here every booking names its pool and lanes.
  • Lane reservations by phone. To reserve a lane you call the front desk, and the line is answered only in the afternoon and evening. Here swimmers can see the open lanes before they call.
  • Lessons only inside a booking widget. Swim lesson times live in the Mindbody widget, day by day. Here they can sit on the same calendar as lap swim.
  • Teams off the page. The Jaguar teams, SOL Aquatics swim, water polo, dive and masters all practice here, but none of their times are on the site. Here they can be one schedule, so lap swimmers know what's coming.
  • Holiday hours as a growing list. The Contact page lists closures going back to 2023. Here a closure appears on its day and disappears when it's past.
