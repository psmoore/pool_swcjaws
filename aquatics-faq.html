Aquatics FAQ

Have a question about what we offer in our Aquatics program and what you can expect at JAWS?

Can I make a swimming lane reservation in advance?

Yes. Call the front desk to reserve, then stop by to pick up a swimming lane pass. The lane-by-lane calendar below shows which lanes are booked.

Can I rent out a pool for a special event?

Yes, through Jaguar Aquatics Wellness & Sports. Email jperez3@swccd.edu or swcswim@swccd.edu.

Do you have lockers?

Day-use lockers are free; items can't be left overnight. Monthly locker rentals are $10/month at the Front Desk.

Is the pool heated?

Yes, 74–80°F.

Will there be a lifeguard on duty?

Always. No one is allowed in the water without a certified lifeguard on duty.

Does Southwestern College have a swim club or Masters program?

Yes: SOL Aquatics, with swim, water polo, dive and masters.

Is there a SWC JAWS app?

Yes, free on Google Play and the App Store, for booking classes, checking schedules and managing your membership.
